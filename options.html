Configuration page for miniGestures

A gesture is a combination of directions. The available directions are U (up), D (down), L (left), R (right).

For example, enter D as the gesture for "Scroll to Bottom" to trigger it with a downward swipe, or DR for "Close Current Tab" to use a down-then-right swipe.

After changing a setting, click the "Save Configuration" button below to apply it.
